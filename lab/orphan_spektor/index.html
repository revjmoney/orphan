INTERSEKTOR · UART MITM BRIDJ

    
     OUTPUTS OFF

   
Cut the line between device A and device B. A's TX to J6.5, J6.11 to A's RX; B's TX to J7.5, J7.11 to B's RX; all grounds together. 3.3 V only, 330 ohm in series on the two outputs.
    SPEKTOR's CH0, CH1, CH4, CH5 see all four wires at once. Frames: 8N1. OFF = listen only · WIRE = bit-level pass-through (any baud or format) · RELAY = byte rewrite in the FPGA · HOLD = the ARM rewrites (sequences, hand edits).

Text with \r \n \t \xNN, or hex:de ad. A one-byte find (to one byte, or to nothing = drop) runs in the FPGA in RELAY, at wire speed. Longer finds work in HOLD: the ARM rewrites them (a partial match waits up to 30 ms).
