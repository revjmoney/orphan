L3+ boards (Scrypt)

L3+ boards run at their PIC's own voltage (stock never sets one) and cut out at 86 °C. A header becomes an L3+ header with Type = L3+ and SET TYPE. Before the first L3+ board goes on this control board: check its cable pinout.

CHIP TEST

Chip map · 24 rows x 3 (address order)

answering / test okweak (test)missing / dead / badoff (100 MHz)unknown (power off)

Select a chip or a row

Works are held for about a second while the clocks change, then each chip's cores are opened again and a fresh work goes out. OFF = the lowest clock (100 MHz); the miner and the chip test count those chips out. A row of 3 may not run more than 25 % away from the board average. The map shows the last chip test until the board powers off; chip numbers are 0..71 (address = 3 x chip).

The curve runs the fan until a slider moves (this one or the FAN slider at the top); CURVE goes back to it. Without a temperature reading for 10 s the fan goes to 100 % by itself.

The power timer turns every board off when nobody renews it. Off: the temperature cuts (S9 90 °C, L3+ 86 °C), the no-temperature cut and the PIC heartbeat still guard the boards.
