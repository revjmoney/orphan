Roots: the SHA-256 hardware is Ardzy's 13_sha256 lab by chryso (GPL-3.0): 4 double-SHA-256 units at 100 MHz. Orphan turned that lab into a real miner: Stratum v1, coinbase and merkle root, extranonce rolling, every result re-checked on the ARM, fan control. At about 1 MH/s it is a lottery miner for solo pools; every share it sends is real work.

An abstract floorplan drawn from the build report and the live registers: not a die photo and not the real placement. Lime bursts cross the AXI bus each time the ARM writes a new midstate and header tail into the FPGA; orange ones come back when the FPGA hands back a nonce. Each unit does two compressions of 64 rounds (2 clocks a round); the sweep is slowed about a million times so it can be seen, and its speed follows the measured clock.
