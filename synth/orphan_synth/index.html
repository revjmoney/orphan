Lights

D5..D8 run in the FPGA (hardware PWM). The front LED flashes green on notes and arp steps, red on the downbeat.

Pitch bend

Play with the mouse/touch (higher on a key = louder), or the computer keyboard: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K = one octave from C, Z / X = octave down / up. A MIDI keyboard on this computer works too (Web MIDI, Chrome), or over the network (rtpMIDI, "Orphan Synth").
