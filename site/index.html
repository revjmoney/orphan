In Bitcoin, an orphan block is one the chain left behind. Orphan is for the control boards the mining world left behind: an S9 controller (Zynq ARM + FPGA, a few dollars used) becomes a hacker's bench computer with game consoles, a radio, a synth, a logic lab, a chip emulator, a miner and a language model built inside the FPGA, and a desktop in your browser. Flash one card and boot. Pick a card, or watch the 2 minute demo.
